# Sthitadhi Story Mode

A 3D voxel portfolio that tells my story in 11 chapters. Scroll to travel through the world, or press **W A S D** to explore it on foot with mouse look, survival mode, animals and a walk-in building for every project.

## Chapters

| # | Chapter | Building |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | SRM Institute of Science and Technology | SRM Tech Park, with a walk-in lobby |
| 2 | AARUUSH techno-management fest | Festival stage |
| 3 | IEEE GRSS SRM | Observatory |
| 4 | CNN on CIFAR-10 and diabetes prediction | Vision Lab |
| 5 | NanoExchange, a C++17 matching engine | Trading floor |
| 6 | PharmaIQ, AI decision intelligence | PharmaIQ lab with five model exhibits |
| 7 | GNN fraud detection | Graph vault |
| 8 | JobPulse | Lighthouse and control room |
| 9 | Open source (PowerToys, OMRChecker) | Contributors hall |
| 10 | Desified and ELEVATE | Store and office |
| 11 | Skills and contact | The summit |

## Controls

- **Story mode:** scroll or use the arrow keys.
- **Explore mode:** W A S D to walk, mouse to look, Space to jump, Shift to sneak, double-tap W to sprint, left-click to break, right-click to place or read a screen, E for inventory, F5 or V to change the camera, Esc for the menu.
